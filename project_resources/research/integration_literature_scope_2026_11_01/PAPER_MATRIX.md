# Paper-by-paper evidence matrix

Read the [synthesis](REVIEW.md) for the proof and conclusion. This matrix covers 64 selected papers; it is not an exhaustive bibliography. **M/T** means relevant primary methods, arguments or theorem statements were inspected. **Limited** means abstract/indexed primary evidence. Neither label means experiments were reproduced or every proof was independently verified.

Interpretations below are our assessment of each paper's input contract and target. “Outside the proof” does not mean causal identification is established. Detailed assumptions, publication versions and evidence locators are preserved in [JSON](PAPER_EVIDENCE.json) and [TSV](PAPER_EVIDENCE.tsv).
