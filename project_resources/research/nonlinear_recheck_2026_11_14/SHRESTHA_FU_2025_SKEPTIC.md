# Skeptical primary check: Shrestha and Fu 2025

Primary: https://arxiv.org/html/2411.03755, Assumption 3.2 and Theorems 3.3–3.4, Sections 3.1–3.2, Appendices C–D. Rechecked 2026-11-14.

Assumption 3.2 ranges over all measurable subsets A of C×S that have positive probability in every domain and are not exactly B×S. Take any z₀=(c₀,s₀) in the product, where S contains another point, and A=(C×S)\{z₀}. Its c₀ section is S\{s₀}, so A is not a cylinder B×S. Under the paper's continuous-density domain laws the point z₀ is null, so all domains assign A probability 1. Assumption 3.2 demands at least two domains assign different probabilities, a contradiction. This direct argument addresses the premise as printed; it does not assess whether the authors intended a restricted family of sets or equality modulo null sets, nor establish a theorem under either repair.

Theorem 3.3 invokes Assumptions 3.1–3.2 and Theorem 3.4 inherits them. Thus neither is established as a nonvacuous positive result for the stated atomless setting. Exact distribution matching and exact ℓ0 constraints also differ from finite GAN training; smooth common-coordinate bijection and the absence of a stochastic count kernel do not match our current measurements. No code execution or simulation was performed.
