# Skeptical primary check: Timilsina et al. 2026

Primary: https://arxiv.org/html/2605.17827, Assumptions 3.1 and 3.3, Theorems 3.4 and 3.6, Appendix B. Rechecked 2026-11-14.

Assumption 3.3 repeats the 2025 paper's all-measurable non-cylinder variability condition and does not state an almost-everywhere or restricted-set exception. For atomless domain laws and nontrivial style S, the punctured product A=(C×S)\{(c₀,s₀)} has probability one for every domain yet is not B×S. This violates the demanded domain-probability contrast. Theorem 3.4 explicitly invokes that assumption; its claimed content/style result is not an established nonvacuous atomless class as stated. Generator tangent-space orthogonality is a separate condition, not a repair. Theorem 3.6's content step follows the same upstream identification argument; its robustness conclusions need a separate proof audit under coherent hypotheses.

A restricted or modulo-null condition might avoid this counterexample but is not what was stated and would not by itself establish the theorem. Common smooth observation coordinates and deterministic invertibility are also unverified for disjoint sequencing count assays. No source code was run.
