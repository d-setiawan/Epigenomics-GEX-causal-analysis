# Public dataset shortlist — 2026-10-31

**Better fits exist for testing integration validity, although no replacement has been shown superior for this repository's human-PBMC question.** Prioritize scMTR-seq if optional fully paired external validation is authorized, Paired-Tag for replicated RNA–histone correspondence, and the Bartosovic mouse-brain collection if genuinely unpaired assays are required. All change the biological setting. This is a targeted catalog, not an acquisition or experiment plan; no matrix or sequencing file was downloaded.

| Candidate | Requested RNA + H3K27ac + H3K4me3 | Advantage over the current collection | Main limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **scMTR-seq**, [GSE277778](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE277778), within GSE277856 | All three measured in the **same cells**, with additional marks | Held-out joint measurements can directly test correspondence and observed cross-assay dependence | Expanded evidence option; stem-cell differentiation/embryos, not PBMC; multiplexing background and replicate structure still matter |
| **Paired-Tag**, [GSE152020](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE152020) | RNA paired with **one histone target per nucleus**; both requested marks available | Each mark has an observed RNA partner and biological replicates | Expanded evidence option; does not observe H3K27ac and H3K4me3 together in one nucleus; mouse brain |
| **Single-cell CUT&Tag mouse brain**, [GSE163532](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE163532): GSE157637 + GSE163484 | Separate unpaired RNA and requested histone assays | Common study, defined sorting/age metadata, replicated histone experiments | RNA P21 versus preferred histone P25; no true pairs and no resolution of residual-coupling ambiguity |

**1. scMTR-seq: strongest optional evidence for the exact three-modality problem.** Human H9 definitive-endoderm differentiation profiles five histone marks, IgG and RNA through days 0–3; mouse E4.5 blastocysts add H3K9me3. The paper reports 31,827 endoderm cells and 5,705 blastocyst cells after its QC. It also discusses residual off-target signal near active chromatin and very sparse blastocyst H3K27me3. These are assay limitations to retain, not reasons to assume that paired observations are error-free. [Wang et al., 2025, DOI 10.1126/sciadv.adu3308](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12327464/)

GEO contains 24 single-cell library records: eight human DNA/RNA sublibrary pairs and four mouse pairs. For example, [GSM8529544](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM8529544) supplies separate H3K27ac/H3K4me3 fragment files; its corresponding [GSM8529552](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM8529552) supplies RNA barcodes/features/MatrixMarket counts. The mouse analogue is [GSM8529560](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM8529560)/[GSM8529564](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM8529564). Raw reads are linked in SRA; processed per-target fragments and RNA matrices are public. GRCh38 and mm10 are used for human and mouse samples respectively. Use **GSE277778**, not the bulk-RNA subseries GSE277699.

Eight or four sublibraries must not be called eight or four independent biological replicates. The article reports replicate concordance for embryos, but the retrieved sample metadata do not establish a donor/culture/embryo-to-sublibrary key sufficient for independent-replicate claims. Reconcile that key and antibody/timepoint barcode maps before defining splits. The strongest potential use is a three-modality benchmark with true pairs withheld from training; it is not automatically an allowed current input or a causal-ground-truth dataset.

**2. Paired-Tag: strong pairwise validation with explicit biological replication.** Adult mouse frontal cortex and hippocampus include RNA co-profiled with H3K27ac, H3K4me3, H3K4me1, H3K27me3 or H3K9me3. The paper states two mice per histone assay and four mice for the combined RNA data; sample/replicate identity is encoded in the first-round barcode. Those statements do not prove that the same individual mice contributed every histone mark. [Zhu et al., 2021, DOI 10.1038/s41592-021-01060-3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7954905/)

All 102 GEO library records reconcile to the series ID list. Representative H3K4me3 DNA/RNA libraries are [GSM4874890](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM4874890)/[GSM4874891](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM4874891). Earlier multiplexed H3K4me1/H3K27ac library records include [GSM4598815](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM4598815)/[GSM4598816](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM4598816); barcode metadata are needed to separate targets. The series lists `Paired-Tag_H3K27ac_DNA_filtered_matrix`, `Paired-Tag_H3K4me3_DNA_filtered_matrix` and `Paired-Tag_RNA_filtered_matrix` archives, with raw reads at [SRP266461](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sra?term=SRP266461). Brain analyses use mm10; the series also contains human/cell-line and Paired-seq accessibility libraries, which must not be silently pooled into the candidate.

This can test RNA↔each-mark state transfer using a genuine RNA counterpart. It cannot directly validate the full RNA/H3K27ac/H3K4me3 joint distribution or cross-mark residual dependence because both marks are not measured in the same nucleus. Region, biological replicate and barcode-collision/QC metadata remain necessary; published coverage summaries are not a substitute for inspecting the selected processed pool.

**3. Bartosovic collection: conditional choice for a genuinely unpaired benchmark.** The histone subseries [GSE157637](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE157637) contains P25 Sox10-Cre/GFP-positive H3K4me3 replicates [GSM4771908](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM4771908)/[GSM4771909](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM4771909) and H3K27ac replicates [GSM4771916](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM4771916)/[GSM4771917](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM4771917), plus other marks/TFs. RNA [GSE163484](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE163484) contains two P21 GFP-positive 10x 3′ libraries, [GSM4979874](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM4979874)/[GSM4979875](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM4979875). The paper reports biological replication of the brain histone experiments; the RNA library labels alone do not establish individual-level matching. [Bartosovic et al., 2021, DOI 10.1038/s41587-021-00869-9](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7611252/)

Processed histone matrices/fragments and RNA count H5s/metadata are public, with raw reads at [SRP281199](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sra?term=SRP281199) and [SRP298453](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sra?term=SRP298453); mm10 is the reference. Complete metadata reconcile 17 histone and two RNA sample records. Restrict any future comparison to the documented GFP-positive population; P15/GFP-negative and cell-line experiments are additional conditions. P21 versus P25 is a real developmental difference, so forced equality of population proportions would be unjustified. This offers clearer cohort structure, not known pairing truth or guaranteed higher sensitivity than the current data.

For either paired candidate, an expanded-evidence protocol would split biological units before masking modalities where metadata support that split, keep paired evaluation identities unavailable to training, use disjoint training cell pools for an explicitly unpaired experiment, and freeze preprocessing/selection before revealing evaluation pairs. If biological units cannot be resolved, describe only an internal cell holdout. Paired measurements can assess observed coupling; they do not establish causal direction. No such benchmark or data intake was executed here.

The bounded search also checked CoTECH, Droplet Paired-Tag and uCoTargetX. They are not added as extra recommendations: the checked uCoTargetX RNA examples use H3K27ac with H3K27me3 or RUNX1, whereas its H3K4me3/H3K27ac combinations are histone-only; a series containing all names is insufficient evidence of the required joint assay. [Xiong et al., 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10796078/)

Exact GEO SOFT endpoints, primary-paper identifiers, retrieval hashes, file links and sample-count reconciliation are preserved in [data_catalog_provenance.json](search_artifacts/data_catalog_provenance.json). Access was unauthenticated on 2026-11-01 UTC; the retrieval was targeted rather than exhaustive. Repository listings establish availability, not verified matrix contents, paired-barcode joins, experimental quality or a demonstrated method ranking.
