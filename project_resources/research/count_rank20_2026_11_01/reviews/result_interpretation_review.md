# Independent interpretation of the dimension-ceiling follow-up

The [independent metric replay](extension_metric_audit.json) passed all 54 sealed arms with the predeclared helper unchanged. It reconstructed 324 within-assay views, 216 real cross-assay pair/stage/panel views, 540 simulated-state matrices, 648 literal paired-bootstrap checks and 43,200 conditional error identities. The maximum numerical difference was $1.17\times10^{-15}$ after rounding upward. No learner or evaluator was imported, and no source model was refitted for that audit. Its full bootstrap scope and limits are recorded in the receipt.

The follow-up removed the computational truncation present in the original unit-exposure 300-feature fits. All 108 original-data assay fits used their selected dimensions of 7–19, and no fit bound the new ceiling of 20. The 54 control assay fits selected rank zero. All 54 arm records and 162 assay records completed successfully; this includes the rank-zero control fits.

Twenty-five within-assay source instances passed the gates across 12 original-data arms: 21 in the linear-chain world and four in the collider world. These are repeated fitted instances across settings and seeds. No real-data assay supplied an eligible source. There were no equivalent signed moment pairs, no unique matches, no all-assay matches, and no extracted graph. CF was again not assessed because the prerequisite moment candidate sets were empty.

The original rank ceiling was therefore a genuine limitation of the first experiment, but removing it did not yield supported cross-assay recovery in this bounded follow-up. This is stronger than reporting only cap-limited abstention. It does not establish that every high-dimensional source model fails, that the observations are biologically independent, or that cross-assay recovery is universally impossible.

Every real cross-assay prediction remained the empty-candidate zero default at both matching stages. Equal performance to the zero baseline is expected from that default and must not be described as recovery of absent relationships. Within-assay covariance changes after raising dimension assess a different target. They can be evaluated on the full panel and the unchanged 30-feature anchor, but they do not supply cross-assay source correspondence or causal directions.

This experiment was designed after inspection of the original study's fit and evaluation records. Selection used the complete family affected by the training-rank ceiling, retained both seeds and all controls, and changed no other acceptance threshold. It remains an adaptive development sensitivity on reused cohorts, not independent validation. The empirical ranks are not known biological dimensions, and the known-kernel model assumptions remain unverified for these data. The [population-identification note](../../count_extensions_2026_11_01/reviews/kernel_population_identifiability.md) further distinguishes finite estimator failure from loss of all population information within an assay.
