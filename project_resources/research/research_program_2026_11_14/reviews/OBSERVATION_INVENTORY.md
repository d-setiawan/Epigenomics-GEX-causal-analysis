## Boundaries and open checks

The study-level inventory is complete for the named current families, not all individual runs. `project_resources/archive/`, legacy scripts, and unrelated older exploratory artifacts were excluded as historical; they must be separately re-audited before reuse. No assessor should infer that every cached run used the same arm, depth field or fitted information solely from this family summary: pin the exact manifest, configuration, input hashes and transductive access. The S1 feature-selection and inherited joint-selection mechanisms do not prove common population support, transportability, or a known selection probability. CA-BOSS uses truth on *other blocks* for its recorded empirical-null calibration and therefore does not support an unqualified real-data label-free claim. The exact statistical law under finite S1 partitioning and the source of any simulated clustering representation remain model-specific review questions. The finite binary rational core has been independently reviewed and kernel checked; general local kernels, shared-stratum completion and finite-sample dataset laws remain unformalized. See `project_resources/research/research_program_2026_11_14/lean/README.md` and `project_resources/research/research_program_2026_11_14/reviews/LEAN_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md`.
