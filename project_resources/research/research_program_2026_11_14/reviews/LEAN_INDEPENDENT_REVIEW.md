## Checks and correspondence

- `Model` is a nonnegative rational 2×2 probability table summing to one. `obs` reports both Bernoulli margins and five fixed metadata/guidance fields. The countermodels agree on every such field. Depths and metadata are deterministic natural numbers, not their joint or conditional distributions; arbitrary assay-local kernels, count matrices and selection are not formalized. Thus this is a valid restricted example, not a proof that the full biological observation map coincides with the toy map.
- The four-outcome functions explicitly give X=U, Y=V and X=U, Y=X. `structural_pushforward` links the two equations to their defined response variables, and `mass` produces the tables in `independent` and `dependent`. Inspection confirms uniform enumeration gives the claimed 1/4 versus 1/2 joint cell and common 1/2 margins. `different_effect` evaluates both interventions, and `different_direct_parent` uses variation of the structural equation while holding the exogenous index fixed. `scm_nonidentification` connects the two indexed equations and observed models. This is an explicit finite functional SCM, not generic SCM semantics or a universal causal assertion.
- The eight-outcome XOR equation has nonzero mass 3/8,1/8,1/8,3/8 and interventional contrast 1/2; all enumerations and equal-observation results are kernel checked. Independence of the bit and noise follows by direct enumeration, but there is no standalone general theorem of exogenous independence, no separate indexed structural negative theorem for the positive example, and the independent comparator's zero effect is established separately. Those are scope limits, not errors in the displayed calculations.
- `frechet` proves both directions: the upper/lower inequalities are necessary and for every rational r in the interval it constructs a valid normalized table. This includes sharp endpoints. It is unconditional, binary and rational, unlike the general finite-stratum real-probability statement proposed in THEORY_CORE. P01's detection bounds and interpretation follow for probability margins (and plug-in empirical margins); a multivariate simultaneous completion does not follow.
- `independence_identifies` assumes the actual equation p11=pX·pY for both models, rather than assuming target equality. For a binary 2×2 distribution it is equivalent to unconditional independence. `independence_completion_exists` constructs an admissible witness for any rational margins in [0,1], and `independence_complete_table` proves all cells agree. Thus the result is not a vacuous uniqueness claim. It does not encode conditional independence given stratum S or prove causal orientation.
- `randomized_countermodel_error` is an algebraic bound for a function assigning an output-one number to each *population observation*. Its mathematical inequality does not require that number lie in [0,1]; interpreting it as a probability adds that external condition. It does not formalize stochastic kernels, iid sample datasets, random metadata, or the product-law pushforward. The stronger finite-sample result in THEORY_CORE is a separate informal argument and is correctly excluded by the Lean README.
- Theorem equivalence in `Identifiable` is equality of rational-valued targets; it does not provide a general target equivalence relation or compare whole models. This is sufficient for the stated scalar counterexample.
