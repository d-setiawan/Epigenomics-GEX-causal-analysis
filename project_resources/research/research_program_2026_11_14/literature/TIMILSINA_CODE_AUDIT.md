# Timilsina shared-component official code: static audit

2026-11-14. Inspected read-only source snapshot at commit `4c968cdfbe14c1792575f045d9092b4c50dfa730`; provenance and hashes are in [timilsina_code_provenance.json](sources/timilsina_code_provenance.json). The supplied snapshot had bundled example datasets removed. No source, notebook, fit, generation, or data acquisition was executed. Paths and line numbers below refer to the [official repository](https://github.com/XiaoFuLab/Shared-Component-Analysis) at that commit. This is a focused audit of the synthetic and single-cell routes, not an audit of every application or results claim.

## Objective and supervision

`Synthetic/synthetic_train.ipynb`, code cell 5, constructs two linear encoders and an adversarial witness, with binary-cross-entropy distribution matching, identity/covariance penalties and, when `anchor_nums > 0`, a squared cross-view anchor penalty. Anchors are taken at the same randomly chosen indices in the two generated views. Cell 19 uses one anchor for a three-dimensional shared component and cell 20 uses three; such a finite-weight penalty is not the exact paired hard constraint of Theorem 3 and one anchor does not meet its d_C pairs. The notebook's no-anchor route omits this penalty; the plotted/generated common source is evaluator truth, not an unpaired observation. The same cell prints the random mixing matrices' condition numbers; that numerical diagnostic does not prove the paper's population variability assumption. Notebook comments advise changing seeds or learning rates if the GAN fails to converge; any replication must retain all seeds and failed runs under a frozen protocol.

The single-cell `train.py` lines 116–128 creates two linear encoder matrices and a discriminator. Lines 193–226 alternate discriminator and encoder binary-cross-entropy losses, add soft penalties on ZGZᵀ−I, and add squared differences of explicitly paired anchors when the weight is positive. The covariance matrices G are empirical uncentered second moments from training loaders (`single_cell/load_data.py` lines 25–62). This practical nonconvex/adversarial optimization and penalty do not enforce exactly the population constrained optimization or establish global optimality or theorem assumptions. Its preprocessing and finite tuning are estimator details, not a proof that the original counts follow noiseless linear mixtures.

**The shipped single-cell command is paired-supervised:** `Single cell sequence analysis/run.sh` lines 1–2 passes `--num_anchors 256 --supervised_w 10.0`; `train.py` lines 100–106 and 219–224 load and penalize these anchors. `single_cell/utils.py` lines 179–186 selects identical training indices from ATAC and RNA matrices. This is unavailable to the project learner. The command should not be called an unpaired reproduction. The script also has a zero-anchor branch, but static inspection alone does not certify that an invocation is runnable or faithful without paired data: `load_supervision` still calls its loader, and its treatment of the empty index list differs from the nonempty supervision branch. An independently adapted unpaired pipeline would require its own code and protocol review.

## Data and evaluation caveats

`single_cell/utils.py` lines 121–128 loads shared-cell IDs and treatment-time metadata; lines 131–176 load paired-cell assay matrices, apply `log1p` and fit a `StandardScaler` on the fixed training indices, with identical train/test index sets across assays. `get_all_data_loaders` lines 189–223 builds two separate loaders over those same training rows and treatment labels. Its unsupervised adversarial loss is distributional, but the underlying source dataset and supervision/evaluation facilities use shared-cell information, which is not supplied by the disjoint project training contract. The treatment labels are not used in the shown gradient loss; they enter data loading and diagnostic construction.

On a cache miss, `single_cell/load_data.py` lines 38–95 builds diagnostic arrays from **training** loaders, matches their counts by treatment label, and concatenates label-grouped rows; it does not establish cell-by-cell matching in those arrays. `train.py` lines 233–268 computes cross-array differences and calls `write_knn` on those diagnostic arrays. `single_cell/utils.py` lines 376–414 then classifies indices using the original `TEST_SET`, despite these arrays having been constructed from training rows and then regrouped. Cached `.pt` files may instead contain other prior contents, which were not available to inspect. Consequently the static source does not validate the meaning of its reported “test” metric or a project-compatible paired evaluation; no numerical assertion is made without execution and the original files. The treatment-based grouping is a diagnostic/data path and does not by itself show those labels are in the encoder gradient.

## Disposition

Theorem 1/2 population results remain distinct conditional positive results; code inspection neither disproves them nor establishes their count-data applicability. The official default single-cell command is incompatible with the permitted no-paired-training contract. No direct execution, hyperparameter tuning, synthetic reproduction, or use of treatment/paired data is authorized by this audit. Before any future experiment, specify a valid independent-sample input and evaluation path, a justified measurement model and rank/variability assumptions, supervision-free fitting, held-out truth isolation, and a frozen protocol; no evidence here makes those conditions true. Full source logic outside the identified synthetic and single-cell files remains unaudited.
