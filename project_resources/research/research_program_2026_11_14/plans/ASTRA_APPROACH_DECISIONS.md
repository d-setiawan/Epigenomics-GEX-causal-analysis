# Astra applicability and research decisions

Decision record opened 2026-11-14, before any new implementation. Primary
theorem details and evidence depth belong to the [literature ledger](../literature/THEOREM_LEDGER.md).
This record distinguishes missing observations from unverified assumptions:
an untestable assumption is not automatically false or information-incompatible.
No latent graph will be scored against the separate focal-feature DAG.

## P01 — binary marginal partial identification

Approved new computation under [the frozen plan](P01_PARTIAL_IDENTIFICATION.md).
The exact set of pairwise couplings is the target. Actual sample/QC differences
restrict empirical interpretation; this is not an empirical genomic graph claim.

## P02 — unpaired linear source and latent-graph identification

Sturma's joint-law and latent-graph results are distinct positive results.
Domain marginal laws are permitted. Distinct standardized non-Gaussian source
laws, adequate rank, linear measurement and shared/private graph restrictions
are substantive model assumptions, not additional data. The graph result
additionally needs its partial-pure-child and rank-faithfulness restrictions.
These could identify a joint and anonymous shared latent graph under the
specified model, not a named genomic regulatory graph.

Decision: reuse the existing native reduced replication, source-code review,
all retained seeds/failures, and count applications after integrity checking.
Do not generate new publication-model worlds merely to repeat it. A faithful
native reproduction tests finite estimation under the published generator;
its success does not verify the genomics measurement model. The prior count
applications test a model adaptation, not native theorem conditions. Source
instability and rejected matches do not decide whether population matching is
impossible. New implementation requires a specific gap beyond those studies.

## P03 — known count-kernel inversion and source composition

Known conditionally independent Poisson observation with constant positive
exposure preserves the full within-assay intensity law by transform uniqueness.
The archived derivation extends this to known NB shape and independent known
zero-retention probabilities. Composing an injective observation kernel with
an identifiable intensity model is mathematically legitimate if *both* sets
of assumptions hold. It does not identify an arbitrary cross-assay coupling.

Decision: reuse the known-kernel derivation and independently reviewed
factorial-moment/PGF/count-source experiments. Those finite methods use only
some functionals of the count law; failure does not refute transform uniqueness.
Observed library-depth proxies are not known exogenous exposure. Fixed NB or
zero-mask sensitivities are not identified technical parameters. A new known-
kernel experiment on artificial worlds would not fix these actual-data gaps.
Exact finite algebra checks are admissible if required for an identified proof
gap, but no new benchmark is approved here.

## P04 — scalar unlinked monotone regression and multivariate monotone maps

The candidate target is a specified response function with a specified direction,
independent known noise and monotonicity (or the appropriate cyclic-monotone
map). It is not an inferred graph direction. Separate samples are compatible;
the required structural equation/noise kernel is not established for these
counts. Count measurement noise and the structural regression noise are
different random objects. Arbitrarily choosing one peak/gene and a known noise
law would create the premise that the experiment was meant to assess.

Decision: theorem applicability and incompatibility/assumption audit first.
No actual-data causal regression fit is approved absent a defensible specified
equation and known noise law. A deterministic monotone-quantile fixture can
check a theorem's algebra, but cannot count as a biological reproduction.
If the primary theorem permits a useful count model without these missing
conditions, return it to Astra for a revised frozen plan before implementation.

## P05 — auxiliary-variable nonlinear ICA

The target is a latent representation with the theorem's residual componentwise
or linear sufficient-statistic equivalence. The auxiliary must be measured with
each sample and satisfy conditional-source modulation/variability restrictions.
Assay identity is constant within an assay; inferred clusters cannot simply be
promoted to observed causal auxiliaries. Depth can reflect biology and is not
certified as a valid auxiliary mechanism. Source identification alone does not
identify causal directions.

Decision: record observation/assumption gaps. Do not feed evaluator states,
days, interventions or pairs into training. An observed valid auxiliary would
change applicability, but is not supplied by this research program.

## P06 — simultaneous multiview identification

Paired nonlinear-view, cross-cumulant and tensor results identify components
or measurement maps using a joint view law and their model restrictions.
Unpaired marginals cannot supply those cross-view statistics by assumption.
Within-assay feature groups are jointly observed, so their appropriate latent
model can be considered separately; this does not create cross-assay pairs.

Decision: paired-training versions are information-incompatible. The existing
paired holdout may evaluate predictions but cannot fit these estimators. Do
not substitute pseudo-pairs and call that faithful theorem reproduction.

## P07 — identifiable finite mixtures and weight-based class alignment

Candidate composition: identify each assay's finite mixture from three or
more conditionally independent within-assay blocks under the exact primary
rank/independence theorem; align classes through distinct common mixture
weights. This composition must be labeled our mathematical application unless
a primary result states it. Repeated weights leave an explicit permutation
ambiguity. Distinct weights do not alone determine residual cross-assay
dependence; a shared-class product joint requires conditional independence
across assays too.

Decision: currently unresolved model applicability, not universal impossibility.
The finite-class, block-independence and rank assumptions have not been
established for continuous biological variability or sparse counts. Different
local-QC pools need not retain equal class weights. Fitting an arbitrary finite
mixture and matching weights would therefore test an unsupported surrogate,
not faithfully reproduce a justified genomics identification theorem. No fit
approved until those conditions and an estimand are justified from permitted
inputs. Primary theorem verification and a deterministic label-collision
counterexample are appropriate next checks.

## P08 — shared linear components from unpaired distributions

The Timilsina shared-component result has a different target from Sturma:
a common subspace/representation, with its specified linear ambiguity and
additional conditions for alignment. Its modality-variability, linear-view
and rank assumptions require exact inspection. A recovered subspace cannot
be treated as named sources or a causal graph. Existing moment whitening is
not automatically a faithful reproduction of this theorem's estimator.

Primary assumptions inspected and focused code audit completed: assess count-layer
compatibility and whether the existing cached worlds instantiate the required
model. Do not dismiss this distinct result merely because Sturma matching
failed; do not implement an arbitrary count adaptation without the approved
model and readout. Record a justified incompatibility or design a separate
frozen existing-input experiment if applicability survives.

Primary-source follow-up decision: Theorem 1's removal of private components
uses modality variability and noiseless full-column-rank linear mixtures.
Its common-coordinate conclusion additionally uses either its non-Gaussian
independent/distinct asymmetric components or its specified hyperrectangle-
support alternative. Theorem 2 instead requires the same mixing matrix and
encoder across views; different genomic feature blocks do not supply this
homogeneous measurement design. Theorem 3 explicitly introduces at least d_C
paired training samples, which are prohibited here. These are three distinct
claims and are not interchangeable. The count source model would require
an objective on identified intensity distributions, not a direct application
of distribution matching to noisy counts. That estimator and sufficient
assumptions are unresolved dependencies, not disproved possibilities. A
faithful published synthetic reproduction would require additional worlds,
which are not authorized and is not justified by a gap in our biological
inputs. No direct-count reproduction is approved. The [focused official-code audit](../literature/TIMILSINA_CODE_AUDIT.md) inspected the actual synthetic and single-cell objectives. The shipped single-cell command uses paired training anchors and is incompatible with this contract; no execution or blanket audit of all source paths was performed.

## P09 — shared covariates, exclusions and conditional independence

A genuinely common measured stratum plus an explicit cross-assay conditional-
independence restriction identifies the product conditional joint. Valid
instrument/exclusion and rank restrictions can identify specified regression
effects in other fusion models. The common variable must actually be measured
comparably in both selected populations; genomic annotation is not a shared
cell covariate. Marginally untestable conditional independence or exclusion
must remain an assumption.

Decision: formalize a small positive conditional-independence core with all
assumptions explicit, but do not apply it using hidden simulation states or
day labels. These true common cell strata/instruments are not currently
available to the permitted learner. This positive theorem identifies a
statistical completion, not a causal graph without further restrictions.

## P10 — optimal transport and guidance

Existence/uniqueness of an optimal coupling or map is identification *within an
assumed transport model*, not identification of the biological coupling from
marginals alone. A specified correct cost/monotonicity restriction could select
a completion, but genomic proximity and embedding distances do not establish
that assumption. Fixed guidance is part of the observation map and can rule
out countermodels only when it truly encodes those substantive restrictions.

Decision: preserve prior objective audits and exact coupling counterexamples;
inspect any new positive transport theorem for unavailable cost/cross-moment
information. Do not rerun alignment algorithms as a substitute for proving
their selected coupling equals the biological target.

The Slawski–Sen multivariate unlinked estimator targets a convex-gradient
regression with its smoothness/strong-convexity/boundedness and known Gaussian
independent-noise assumptions; the count assays do not supply those structural
conditions. Its noiseless shuffled-cohort assignment proposition is a separate
observation design from genuinely disjoint training. The Syrota 2026 manifold
preprint's alignment is unique only when the relevant metric-measure
automorphism ambiguity is absent; its GW statement concerns global population
optima, not arbitrary solver stationary points. Neither true common metric
geometry, invertible noiseless view maps, nor the symmetry exclusion has been
established for the sparse count channels. Its target is alignment, not a
genomic graph. A rendered-hypothesis inspection remains unresolved because
the accessed HTML omits some displayed conditions. This source is provisional
and does not support a positive claim for the project's actual inputs.

## P11 — nonlinear content/style recovery with dimension upper bounds

OpenAlex follow-up exposed a distinct positive result: Shrestha–Fu (ICLR
2025), [primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.03755), Theorems 3.3 and
3.4. Astra decision before implementation: **no native application approved**.
Equation 1 uses the same differentiable bijection and the same observed
coordinate space in every domain, with continuous open content/style support,
a common content law, independent content/domain-style blocks and the exact
domain-variability restriction. Separate RNA and histone feature maps are
not observations of this stipulated common mixing map; assigning equal matrix
width does not supply it. Discrete stochastic count observations also do not
instantiate the assumed smooth invertible measurement without a new argument.

Theorem 3.3 assumes known block dimensions and exact population distribution
matching, block independence and an injective differentiable encoder. Its
target is content/style blocks up to injective block transformations. Theorem
3.4 adds dimension *upper bounds*, positive finite joint density and exact
style-support l0 minimization under the population constraints; it does not
say arbitrary unknown dimensions and any sparsity penalty work. The practical
GAN/l1 objective is an approximation, not the theorem's exact optimizer or a
finite-sample consistency guarantee. Neither theorem identifies a genomic DAG.

A faithful native reproduction would test the published common-coordinate
continuous generator and constrained population approximation; no additional
worlds are authorized and existing genomics count worlds do not supply those
conditions. A justified extension would need separate assay measurement maps,
an explicit stochastic count channel, a correct remaining equivalence and a
proof that available marginal information identifies the proposed block target.
No published-model failure or success on a fabricated same-width input would
settle that extension. Exact primary and available-code inspection are recorded
in the literature ledger; no code execution, fitting or new data follows from
this decision. This new result is a genuine restricted positive route, not a
reason to weaken the broad-class counterexample or claim all nonlinear models
impossible.

**Premise-consistency correction after independent review:** these are the
authors' claimed conditional identification results, not an accepted example
of a nonvacuous positive model under the literal printed assumptions. Assumption
3.2 quantifies every measurable non-cylinder set of positive probability. For
continuous domain laws and a nontrivial style space, removing one common null
point from the full support gives such a set with probability one in every
domain, contradicting that requirement. The
[independently checked set argument](../theory/DOMAIN_VARIABILITY_OBSTRUCTION.md)
records exact scope. A restriction modulo null sets or to a smaller set class
would need a repaired statement and proof; neither is assumed here. This
obstruction is separate from the count-design mismatch and does not refute
the practical GAN's empirical utility. It further blocks theorem-based fitting
approval rather than authorizing a fabricated successful example.

## P12 — differential content/style independence

Timilsina and colleagues (2026), [primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.17827),
Theorem 3.4 supplies a distinct proposed relaxation of statistical block
independence: orthogonality of content/style generator-Jacobian subspaces and
full style-Jacobian rank, with the common smooth generative model, distribution
matching and domain variability. The target remains blocks up to invertible
block maps, not a causal graph. Astra decision before implementation: no native
genomics count fit. The common-coordinate smooth measurement and geometric
restrictions are not established for heterogeneous noisy assay observations.

Primary inspection also finds that Assumption 3.3 repeats the same literal
all-measurable non-cylinder variability condition. The common-null-point
obstruction applies under atomless domain laws; Jacobian orthogonality does
not repair it. Thus this is a claimed conditional extension with a premise
consistency problem, not an independently established nonvacuous alternative
for our data. Theorem 3.6 gives an approximate-orthogonality robustness claim:
content recovery and a derivative bound on content leakage into learned style,
involving the principal-angle error and a learned style-Jacobian singular
value. It is not a finite-sample/global-optimization guarantee, and its proof
uses the same content-identification step rather than repairing variability.

Faithful experimentation would first require a mathematically clarified
variability condition and a stated relation between the count observations
and its differential model. No new worlds are authorized to obscure these
dependencies. Official code may be inspected to distinguish its actual loss
from idealized constraints, but execution is not an identification argument.

## Acceptance across approaches

Each final cycle must record attempted work, proved/reproduced/measured result,
needed assumptions, observed failures and competing explanations, contribution
to identification, and justified next step. A missing proof or unsupported
biological premise is an unresolved dependency, not evidence of no biology.
All positive-result decisions are revisited after primary-source verification
and independent adversarial review; approval of a theory audit is not blanket
approval to fit a model using forbidden information.
