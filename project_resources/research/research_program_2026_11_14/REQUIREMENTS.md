## Operating constraints

Only existing permitted observations enter fitting or tuning. Paired measurements,
simulation truth, state labels and causal labels are evaluation-only where the
existing contract allows them. They cannot select sources, hyperparameters or
stopping rules. Deterministic algebra fixtures are labeled and are not benchmark
worlds. Historical files, protocols and predictions are preserved. No emojis.

At most two Terra workers run alongside Astra and the root execution interface.
Queued perspectives are literature/theorem verification, identification and
counterexamples, implementation/simulation, Lean, and independent adversarial
review. Model availability and any environmental limitation must be reported.
