# Project mission

**Determine which relationships among nearby histone-mark peaks and a gene’s expression can be learned when each assay measures different cells.** The long-term biological question is how regulatory elements act together at a locus. Our immediate task is to distinguish the part of that structure supported by existing measurements from the part supplied by an integration model. [Research recap](../reports/2026-11-22-unpaired-assay-recap.md) · [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md)

## What the observations give us

In the original PBMC analysis, each chromatin assay measures one histone mark. RNA comes from a separate experiment. Peaks from the same assay can be measured together. A peak from one assay and expression from another have no observed cell-by-cell pairing. The chromatin hashtags record technical groups. A common donor or biological sample identifier across the experiments has not been verified. The separate scMTR-seq S1 pilot withholds same-cell measurements for evaluation and supplies disjoint cells to the learner. [PBMC sources](../../pipelines/empirical/data/results/data_sources.md) · [S1 cohort](../../pipelines/empirical/data/scmtr_seq/README.md)

| Scientific target | Evidence available here |
| --- | --- |
| Within-assay dependence | Joint measurements of peaks within an assay support association and conditional-dependence tests under their sampling assumptions. Direct causation requires further assumptions. |
| Cross-assay variation carried by shared state | With a justified correspondence between states, we can ask whether average chromatin signal and average expression rise in the same states. This is an assay–assay relationship across states. |
| Within-state, same-cell dependence | Given a cell’s state, does unusually high chromatin signal coincide with unusually high expression in that cell? Unpaired marginal distributions leave several answers possible. |
| Local causal adjacency or direction | A stable association, an externally supported peak–gene link or an arrow supplied by a prior can motivate a regulatory hypothesis. Each has a different evidential basis from a causal effect identified by the observations. |

## Why integration and aggregation are separate questions

Averaging all variables over the same cells with the same fixed weights preserves a linear structural equation. Arbitrarily pairing two averages from independent cell samples can erase their cross-assay covariance, even if both assays sample the same population. Matching state can restore variation in state means. It still leaves open how individual deviations from those means co-occur. That missing part can change conditional independences and hence the fitted graph. We test what integration recovers before comparing aggregation strategies. ## What the project has established

**At biological loci.** In the historical monocyte panel, cross-mark adjacencies that survived changes in the metacell partition were enriched for contacts with a shared promoter. No cross-mark direction was stable across the partitions tested. In a count-calibrated simulation, the same selection rule enriched for peaks with a shared latent driver. This supports studying shared regulatory context. The identity of a driver and the direction of an individual biological edge remain open. [Empirical result and simulation controls](../../pipelines/simulation/aggregation_identifiability/results/crossmark_identifiability.md) · [External evaluation](../../pipelines/empirical/evaluation/README.md)

**Under an explicit model.** Suppose each assay samples the same distribution of independent, non-Gaussian latent sources with distinguishable distributions. More precisely, after scaling each source to unit variance, their joint distribution has no nonidentity signed-permutation symmetry. If each assay uses a full-column-rank linear map from those sources to its log intensities and samples counts through a conditional product-Poisson model, the population laws identify a linear map between the supported log-intensity subspaces. Exponentiating gives the transport between assay intensities. With the same latent draw for the hypothetical shared cell and independent count noise across assays given that draw, they also specify a count joint. The result allows different numbers of features per assay. [Square model and assumptions](nonlinear_repair_2026_11_15/theory/COMP_COUNT_SHARED_TRANSPORT.md) · [Unequal-width corollary and review](nonlinear_repair_2026_11_15/theory/COMP_TALL_MATRIX_COROLLARY.md)

**In finite samples.** A raw-count estimator satisfied the covariance, transport and predictive criteria on every reused world generated under the tested one- to three-source binary subclass. It received the source dimension, learned the source probabilities and chose among starts using unpaired calibration counts. Paired cells and the generating parameters were used for assessment and diagnostics. An earlier test on background features from two cached, biologically motivated simulations did not meet its recovery criteria. Whether biological assays meet the model assumptions, whether source dimension can be estimated reliably and whether peak–gene directions are recoverable remain separate questions. ## How we judge progress

- State the observation unit, the target and which information is available during fitting. Reserve paired evaluation cells, simulator truth and held-out biological labels for their declared roles. [Observation and target contract](research_program_2026_11_14/theory/OBSERVATION_TARGET_CONTRACT.md)
- Test shared-state recovery against controls that can fail, including interchangeable sources, sparse counts and arbitrary couplings. Report results separately for within-assay signal, across-state transport and causal edges. Reuse the available simulation worlds and record when prior inspection makes a comparison exploratory. [Simulation tracks](../../pipelines/simulation/README.md)
- Assess biological plausibility with target-gene-aware predictions, genetic evidence and promoter contacts. Distinguish evidence supplied as a prior from evidence used to evaluate a prediction. [Empirical evaluation](../../pipelines/empirical/evaluation/README.md) · [Limitations](LIMITATIONS.md)
- Credit the ingredients of the corollary, including Poisson-mixture identification and independent component analysis. Our contribution is to specify the observation model and recoverable target, build an estimator and show where it succeeds and fails. The prior-art audit found substantial precedent for the constituent results. [Novelty audit](count_transport_novelty_2026_11_15/README.md)
