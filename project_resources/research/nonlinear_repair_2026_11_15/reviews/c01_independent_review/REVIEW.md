# Independent C01 implementation and output review

Reviewer: independent of implementation author. 2026-11-15. This review does not approve threshold changes or establish population assumptions. Frozen source files were not modified.

## Source and numerical checks

Read the signed plan, protocol, source approval, `model.py`, `train.py`, `evaluate.py`, and tests. Five sealed deterministic tests passed in the pinned `scrna_r` environment. An independent SciPy fixture (`c01_independent_fixture.py`) checked Poisson and NB2 log masses; numerical optimization of each responsibility-weighted one-feature likelihood checked the NB2 and Poisson M-step; mixture means, variances and zero probabilities agreed with independently calculated expressions. The NB2 implementation has fixed shape 2 and mean μ, giving variance μ+μ²/2. These checks do not prove kernel injectivity or real-data adequacy.

Training uses the hash-sorted disjoint within-training fit/calibration split, computes the column-null separately inside each split, and selects restarts/K using only the permitted training/calibration inputs. The evaluator checks fit/source/input seals before opening heldout data; both original- and null-trained models are scored on the unpermuted test counts. Paired reference joins check feature ordering and exact reconstruction. Test evaluation is not used for matching or optimizer choice. S1 local training QC and all-assay holdout selection differ; comparisons require that caveat. No test-based modification is justified.

`c01_fit_replay.py` independently checked all 24 sealed artifact hashes, the protocol and approval hashes, all 287 available calibration scores, common and separate K choices, convergence traces, all 21 selected-K bootstrap stability/interval/permutation/status decisions. One of the 288 candidate assay/K calibration scores was unavailable due to nonconvergence. `c01_em_replay.py` separately implements the likelihood and EM updates and reproduces selected RNA fits plus first two bootstrap warm starts in three 30-feature arms: linear-chain Poisson original, S1 NB2 original, and collider Poisson column-null. This is representative, not a rerun of every optimization trajectory. All candidate-assignment sets are empty, so candidate completion/range values are unobserved in C01; the original deterministic tests independently check completion algebra on a fixture, rather than demonstrating a realized fitted completion.

## Training findings

- 21 of 24 arms abstained at K>1; the other three were S1 column-null K=1 baselines. No arm produced a shared-state assignment or conditional cross-assay covariance candidate. All 16 simulation arms chose the upper grid boundary K=4; this is not evidence K=4 is the true biological state number. In all eight S1 arms each histone assay separately prefers K=1; RNA prefers K=4 in six and K=2 in the two 300-feature null arms.
- Of 864 primary restarts, 839 converged and 25 reached the iteration cap. Of 4,032 bootstrap candidates, 3,883 converged, 101 reached the cap, and 48 failed with dead components. Nine of 2,016 bootstrap resamples had no converged candidate. Among 282 assay/K cells having at least two converged restarts, 155 had a final average log-likelihood spread exceeding 1e-5; the largest spread was approximately 3.803. This records local optimizer sensitivity, not a global-optimum test.
- Across 63 selected-K>1 assay blocks, 17 pass the complete stability gate; 15 have fewer than 26 valid bootstraps, 11 fail point effective-membership, 31 fail displacement and 16 fail weight-uncertainty where calculable. These categories overlap, and displacement/uncertainty are only evaluated after adequate valid replicates and point membership.
- The linear-chain 300-feature Poisson original arm passes all three assay stability gates, yet no global weight assignment is compatible. Its H3K27ac component interval [0.557, 0.628] does not overlap any RNA interval (maximum upper endpoint about 0.462). Hence its abstention is a cross-assay weight-compatibility failure, not a failure of the three within-assay stability gates.
- For S1 300-feature original Poisson RNA, a fitted responsibility has correlation +0.779 with the observed depth proxy; this is descriptive and does not distinguish technical exposure, cell size and biology. Null controls retain original metadata after column permutation, breaking the original count-depth relationship. Other source diagnostics and profiles remain in the sealed fits.

## Evaluation review

Root ran the authorized evaluator once; evaluation seal SHA-256 is `ed6fcb6c3a1002e851e374a20f90dc08b99a809d952f1dd6b81bee32ce45d91b`. The independent `c01_evaluation_replay.py` checks all 24 evaluation output hashes and all heldout input hashes, independently recomputes every 216 within-assay metric records (including K1 and common30 views), 384 simulated responsibility/state correlations, and 36 S1 paired zero-baseline records. It confirms the paired reference reconstructs from the frozen public test rows. The replay passed. Candidate predictions and range coverage are absent for all arms because no assignment passed; there is no fitted candidate for the paired evaluator to score.

For the panel-sized within-assay test views, original S1 has better selected-mixture log score than its K1 baseline in 6 of 12 assay/arm comparisons and worse in 6; S1 column-null has 1 better, 9 equal (three K1 arms times three assays), 2 worse. In the two cached simulation datasets, original arms improve log score in all 24 assay/arm comparisons and reduce standardized off-diagonal covariance RMSE in all 24; the column-null arms still improve log score in 23 of 24, but reduce off-diagonal covariance RMSE in only 9 of 24. These are descriptive, correlated views of a small fixed study, not independent significance tests. The full 300 and common30 views have different feature dimensions, and the S1 paired evaluation population has different QC selection from training.

No implementation defect was found in the checked likelihoods, frozen selection/gating decisions or replayed evaluation metrics. The absence of a candidate rules out a claim of validated cross-assay covariance recovery; it does not establish the absence of shared biological factors.

## Provenance and limitations

The reviewed fit seal SHA-256 is `dd42e7daefa51695a4e94fdb365a51057e828087a0cb490462e20797be326586`. Scripts and their digests are in this directory. Checks establish consistency with the declared implementation on these inputs, not that the assumptions of common selected-population weights, product count emissions, or cross-assay conditional independence hold. Count likelihood improvement, stability, cross-assay compatibility and causal interpretation are different claims. The bootstrap is conditional on selected K, and the optimizer search and score thresholds are finite and fixed.
