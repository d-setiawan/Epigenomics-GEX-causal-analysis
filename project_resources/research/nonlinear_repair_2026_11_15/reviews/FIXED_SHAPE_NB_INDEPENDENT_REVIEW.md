# Independent review: fixed-shape negative-binomial bridge

2026-11-15. Verdict: **the stated population injection argument is correct under its stated known-shape product-NB kernel**, assuming the separately reviewed product-Poisson mixing-law injection. This is a prose mathematical review; no Lean result or empirical adequacy claim is made.

For each coordinate, if G~Gamma(shape r, rate r), conditional Poisson rate λG gives PGF E[exp(λG(z−1))]=(1+λ(1−z)/r)^(−r), the NB law with mean λ and variance λ+λ²/r, including λ=0. Independent G coordinates produce the required product conditional kernel. Equality of count-vector distributions therefore gives equality of the law of R=Λ⊙G by the reviewed Poisson injection. Because each G is positive and finite a.s., each coordinate zero pattern is exactly retained; conditioning on a positive-probability pattern is conditioning on a Λ-measurable event and leaves the independent G law unchanged. On the positive coordinates, log R=log Λ+log G. Euler's integral in [NIST DLMF §5.2](https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.2) gives E[e^{it log G}]=r^(−it)Γ(r+it)/Γ(r); the same source states that Γ has no zeros, and r+it avoids its poles. The product characteristic function of the log multipliers is nowhere zero, so division and characteristic-function uniqueness recover each conditional log-rate law. The empty positive pattern is directly known. Finite patterns exhaust the sample space. No rate moment condition is required.

For finite mixtures, unique recovery of the mixing measure identifies distinct atom vectors and their masses; pairwise-distinct exactly common masses then give the same unique cross-assay alignment as the Poisson theorem. A statistical joint still needs an additional common-population and across-assay conditional-independence assumption. The auxiliary-variable theorem can compose with the kernel only when both competing models have the same fixed known shape vector, and its own decoder, positivity and contrast-rank assumptions hold.

C01's fixed shape 2 satisfies the kernel parameter form by design. This does not show actual counts are NB, shapes are known biologically, selection preserves common weights, estimation is stable, the optimizer reaches the global maximum, or any causal graph is identified. The proof does not automatically transfer to unknown/learned shape, dependent observation coordinates, unknown exposure, or endogenous depth. It is a post-fit clarification of population information, not a protocol or threshold change. Nothing here makes a claim about the output of C01 or changes its frozen results.
