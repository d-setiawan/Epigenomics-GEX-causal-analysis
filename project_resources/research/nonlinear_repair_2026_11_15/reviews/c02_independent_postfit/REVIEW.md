## Independent fit replay

`fit_replay.py` verifies all 324 fitted artifact hashes, source and nine train-input hashes, protocol and approval bindings; all 36 applications completed their three-start budget without timeout. It inspects all 108 start records, confirms default parameters, finite raw bounds and author objective ordering for all 90 successful starts, and independently recomputes their full training Poisson log likelihoods and TLP penalties. It verifies selected start, optimizer/stopping-derived status, and independently recomputes all 30 selected full fit and calibration scores, feature scales, positive state counts and estimated-Q certificates. The independent Q implementation checks row-type combinatorics rather than calling the author's helper. All checks pass. This is a parameter/likelihood replay, not rerunning the stochastic optimizer from every seed or a global-optimum certificate.

There are 16 `finite_converged`, 14 `finite_nonconverged` and six `failed` applications. Across all 108 starts, 90 are finite/scorable and 18 fail during attained training likelihood scoring. All 90 successful starts report outer tolerance stopping; 42 have at least one unsuccessful SLSQP call (1,037 unsuccessful calls across 88,230 optimizer calls). Fourteen selected results are correctly flagged `finite_nonconverged` due to such optimizer failures; retaining their metrics is not an assertion of convergence.

`failcheck.py` independently recomputes the nonfinite scoring cause for all 18 failed starts. They comprise all three starts in each of six S1 histone arms. Rates are finite and nonnegative, but the selected thresholded support gives zero probability to positive training observations: 7, 16, 12, 21, 37, or 105 source-fit rows per respective application. There are no NaN score rows or negative rates in these cases. The official author selector requires finite attained objective, so no start can be selected for those six arms. These are meaningful support/optimization failures under the fixed model and are retained; no rate flooring or row deletion was applied.

Fifteen of the 30 finite fitted Q matrices pass the stated estimated-design condition and 15 fail; 29 have four positive fitted latent-state masses and one has three. This is a statement about **estimated** support, not the theorem's unobserved true Q, genericity, latent positivity, causal assumptions or actual identified structure. S1's finite subset has three passes and three failures; its other six applications failed. The maximum absolute stored posterior-responsibility/depth correlation in the 30 finite applications is +0.717 (linear-chain, H3K4me3, 300-feature original); correlation is descriptive, and depth can reflect biological and technical variation. The fixed bounded rate model, row ordering and S1 selection differences remain substantive limitations.

## Independent heldout replay

After the separate evaluator gate, `eval_replay.py` verifies all 36 evaluation output hashes, nine public-test input hashes and both fit-seal hashes, and independently recomputes every reported C02 metric for 46 panel/common30 views and every C01 K=1/K=4 comparator metric for 108 views. All six failed applications remain explicitly present with no C02 metrics; their C01 comparators remain reported. The evaluation seal says no paired/state references were read, and the replay reads none. All 46 C02 scored views have finite log scores and zero zero-probability rows; the v2 representation for -∞ was previously exercised in a deterministic fixture rather than by changing any fitted model. All replayed numbers match.

For the single panel-size view of each of the 30 finite C02 applications, C02 has a better heldout mean log score than the independently fitted C01 Poisson K=1 baseline in 22 and a worse score in eight. Against C01 Poisson K=4 it scores better in one and worse in 29. On S1 specifically, six of 12 applications fail; among the remaining six, one is flagged finite-converged and five finite-nonconverged, four score better than K=1 and one scores better than K=4. These are descriptive, correlated exploratory comparisons with different parameterizations and optimizers, not matched-complexity tests, a causal claim or an identified-state validation. The study used existing data after C01 results were known, and assay-local training QC differs from any common biological population.

No blocking code defect was found in the checked sealed fit decisions or evaluation metrics. The independent replay does not establish universal optimizer correctness, prove the DCRL theorem's assumptions for these data, score causal graphs, establish paired cross-assay recovery, or certify convergence of the 14 flagged applications.
