## Fit replay

`fit_replay.py` verifies all sealed fit/source/input hashes and all 36 application records and 108 start records. It independently reconstructs the fit/calibration split, full-panel within-split column null, source-active features and inactive-feature restoration, fit scales, rate-box/weight bounds, source tau, convergence flags and lowest-final-moment-objective start selection. A separate elementary-symmetric-polynomial calculation recomputes the full 1–4-order source moment objective minus its data-only constant for all 108 stored starts; all match. Its formula also matched the author implementation on an isolated deterministic fixture (6.107627773620355 versus 6.107627773620353). Every selected source-fit and calibration Poisson score summary is independently recomputed, including exact zero-probability handling. All checks pass. `reproduce_one.py` reran one existing-input author optimizer trajectory (S1 RNA, 30 features, original, seed 0) and matched its stored rates, weights, final objective and converged status at iteration 46. Other trajectories and individual QP iterates were not rerun; prefit tests checked QP/KKT on small matrices. No global optimum or genericity follows.

All 36 applications and all 108 starts completed. The selected outputs comprise 13 finite-converged and 23 finite-nonconverged solutions. Six selected mixtures have -∞ source-fit log scores; eight have -∞ calibration scores. These are valid moment-estimator outputs assigning zero probability to observed positive counts, preserved under the frozen protocol with null+status+row count and no flooring or row dropping. Across models, 141 source-fit rows have undefined responsibilities; the saved correlations explicitly condition on supported rows and these counts are not unique-cell totals. All six collider-original, six collider-null and six linear-chain-null selected fits are nonconverged; six of six S1 original and four of six S1 null fits are converged by the author stopping rules. These convergence facts do not imply high-quality recovered biological classes.

The source uses one-row block updates, r=4, orders 1–4, no warmup/acceleration, and at most 100 iterations. Source sample-variance transform, constant-feature removal and source box restriction can affect the model; penalty-free source objective is not a Poisson log likelihood and its omitted data-only constant varies across applications. Lower cost between starts of the same application selects a fit; costs should not be compared across panels or datasets. The fit/test sample and original feature-selection caveats remain.

A separately approved launcher admitted exactly those reviewed libraries while preserving the original numeric evaluator, source and fit files; [runtime amendment review](RUNTIME_AMENDMENT_REVIEW.md) records the independent guard checks. `eval_replay.py` verifies all 36 output and nine public-test hashes, the C01/C02/C03 fit seals, and independently recomputes every metric in all 54 C03, 108 C01 and 46 available C02 panel/common30 views. Six C02 comparison applications correctly remain unavailable. All checks pass. Of 54 C03 views, 42 are finite and 12 have -∞ predictive log scores, with 164 impossible-row occurrences across the views; common30 and 300-feature views overlap, so those are not counts of unique cells. Source/calibration/test rows are retained and -∞ is not replaced by a finite value.

For each of the 36 panel-size views, C03 has a better heldout mean log score than C01 K=1 in 14, a worse finite score in 11, and -∞ in 11. Versus C01 K=4 it is better in two, worse finite in 23, and -∞ in 11. Against C02, ten C03 scores are better, 12 worse finite and eight -∞, with six C02 fits unavailable. These are correlated exploratory comparisons of different models and optimizers, not significance tests or matched-complexity tests. A zero-probability score is a real predictive failure of the selected model on that row, not evidence of nonexistent latent structure.

No source mutation, cross-assay matching or paired/state/causal reference was used. Nothing here establishes that Poisson emissions, conditional independence, genericity or shared population assumptions hold in the real data; neither improved scores nor source convergence establish causal graph recovery.
