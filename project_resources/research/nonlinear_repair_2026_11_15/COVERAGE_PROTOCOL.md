# Expanded nonlinear and count-data identification coverage, 2026-11-15

Question: Which mathematically distinct population identification and estimable model classes can use the existing unpaired assay count marginals and observed assay IDs, and which repaired hypotheses give nonvacuous conclusions? This is a scoping review coupled to primary theorem and proof inspection, not a guarantee that all literature has been located. No new data, synthetic worlds, model fits or paired training observations are authorized in this review. Previous sealed programs remain unchanged.

Search families: (1) nonlinear shared-content/domain variability; (2) noisy, discrete and exponential-family nonlinear ICA; (3) unpaired multimodal shared/private models including Sturma and Timilsina; (4) finite mixture/tensor latent class identification with cross-assay weight alignment; (5) nonparametric Poisson mixing and measurement operator injectivity; (6) sparse influence/geometric nonlinear ICA; (7) partial-observation and multi-view models, tracking joint observations; (8) unpaired domain maps, symmetries and optimal transport; (9) domain-varying mixtures and heterogeneous distributions; (10) count-likelihood single-cell integration methods and their actual theoretical guarantees. Start with prior recheck papers and their backwards/forward references; include methods without the word identifiability if an estimator's exact model potentially satisfies the contract.

Search OpenAlex, supplemented by primary publisher/arXiv and additional indexed searches. Capture query, endpoint, timestamp, sort, page, returned IDs, and raw pages; deduplicate DOI then title/year. Screen titles and abstracts first, assign inclusion or explicit reason, investigate full text/theorem/supplement and official code for shortlisted results. Record candidates with exact source, population target and symmetry, input/selection/measurement requirements, testable implications, estimator and fit requirements, proof and code inspection status, and proposed permitted-existing-input validation. Counterexamples and negative results count as evidence. Assay IDs are observed, but do not imply common generators, latent mask labels, common selection, or interventions. Lack of direct applicability motivates possible repairs, not automatic exclusion.

For each branch follow at least two discovery routes and key backward/forward citations, prioritizing novel assumptions, outcomes and estimators over redundant titles. Record the search interval and continuation boundary, screening counts and gaps, ranking/citation-index bias, and stop only after a documented branch-specific marginal-yield review. No finite search supports a claim of literal exhaustive coverage. Apply chronological rules of the input contract, use no secrets, and communicate all potentially implementable positive candidates for Astra's approval before implementation.
