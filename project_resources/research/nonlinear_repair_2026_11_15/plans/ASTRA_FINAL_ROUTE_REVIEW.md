# Astra route decisions and research-cycle review

2026-11-15. This is the final interpretation ledger, not a retrospective replacement for the frozen prefit plans. The detailed [theorem matrix](../THEOREM_ALGORITHM_DISPOSITION.md) retains exact primary results, assumptions, equivalences and source limitations. [Initial route decisions](ASTRA_ROUTE_DECISIONS.md) and the three experimental plans preceded implementation. The table records what each route contributes and the justified next step without treating absent metadata and unverified biological assumptions as the same obstacle.

| Distinct route | Attempt, result and assumptions | Failure or unresolved explanation; next decision |
|---|---|---|
| Unrestricted nonlinear content/style variability | Prior recheck established literal null-set and finite-atomless simultaneous-bisection obstructions to the stated premise; no new fit based on it. | An a.e. wording change is insufficient. Pursue substantive restricted classes A, C, D and E rather than claim universal nonlinear impossibility. |
| Restricted nonlinear scalar/contrast repairs A and C | Explicit population proofs and nonvacuous examples, independently reviewed. Restricted encoder or known common inverse link and full style-contrast span identify the stated content target. | Those assumptions are not implied by assay labels or disjoint features. A faithful fit would require proving the observation/model correspondence first; no hidden environments introduced. |
| Full-count auxiliary exponential-family repair D | Poisson injection plus conditional RN contrasts identifies sufficient statistics up to affine equivalence; independent proof review and scalar formal core passed. | Common injective decoder and full-rank auxiliary modulation are unverified. Existing assay IDs cannot be relabeled as valid interventions. No count-VAE implementation claimed certified by this theorem. |
| Finite Poisson/NB shared states B; C01 | Approved finite-mixture model, source-only stability and common-weight matching gates executed. Twenty-one multi-state arms abstained; three null single-state arms; no accepted cross-assay match. | Sparse histone counts, K-boundary selection, depth, instability, local optima and unequal selected class weights remain alternatives. Do not relax gates. The target would be aligned state laws/posteriors, with joint completion requiring extra conditional independence. |
| Restricted discrete latent-class DCRL; C02 | Official estimator applied under frozen K=2 latent binary design; independent loading-pattern certificate and full predictive replay. Six failed, 16 finite-converged, 14 finite-nonconverged applications. | Fitted certificate does not verify true pattern assumptions. Zero-support failures and poor comparator scores do not prove nonidentification. No latent-DAG extraction justified by these outcomes. |
| General finite product-mixture moments; C03 | Distinct official baseline estimator, exact constrained-update adapter, frozen moment-objective selection; 36 applications completed and independently replayed. | Many nonconverged fits and zero-probability heldout rows; not a consistency test under guaranteed theorem assumptions. No source alignment or causal graph follows from recovering within-assay mixture parameters. |
| Count-channel identification | Full product-Poisson proof and known-shape NB bridge reviewed; unknown-shape countermodel reviewed. | Known kernel is substantive. Learned dispersion, endogenous total-depth exposure or binary thresholding cannot silently inherit injection. New channels need their own proof before changing the model. |
| Asymmetric independent-source transport E/E2 | Count-law injection and ICA yield nonlinear rate transport; tall matrices and unequal assay widths allowed on identified support subspaces. Independent proof reviews accepted. | This is not excluded merely by different feature meanings. Symmetry-free common sources and log-linear mixing remain unverified; existing CoMP Gaussian-decoder/prior code is not a faithful estimator. Developing a count-native estimator and proving its correspondence is an unresolved dependency, not an empirical success. |
| Jacobian sparsity/scattering, contrastive ICA and piecewise-affine Gaussian mixtures | Primary theorems, supplements and available source inspected; distinct derivative/support/contrast assumptions recorded. Some population count-law compositions are possible under explicit decoder restrictions. | Sparse Jacobians or source-specific observed directions are assumptions on an unknown generative map, not supplied genomic guidance. cICA requires common observed coordinates; PWA proofs and code require a separate count-native estimation correspondence. No arbitrary software stacking. |
| Real-analytic kink-source ICA | Direct forward citation found a new 2026 theorem; exact primary scrutiny and independent review establish a countermodel to the general signed-affine conclusion, while a separate exact-Laplace global analytic rigidity proof and count-law composition pass independent review. | The componentwise countermodel does not refute weaker componentwise equivalence. The restricted count result is within-assay only; independent cross-assay signed permutations remain. No released count-native estimator or justified finite-sample adaptation is available, so implementation remains a documented dependency. |
| Nonparametric clusterable mixtures (Aragam) | Primary conditional identification and constructive clustering route inspected; clusterability/separation and iterated approximation limits recorded. | A count-specific regular approximation family, separation argument and estimator correspondence are not established. Cannot assume sparse overlapping count components satisfy it. |
| Multi-view/tensor and shared-anchor/feature methods | Within-assay independent blocks can support finite-mixture results above. Joint-view methods and multi-study anchor theorems retain their exact observation requirements. | Paired views, known anchors and shared measurement coordinates are not acquired by shuffling cells or mapping feature names. Existing guidance can constrain models but does not create a joint law. |
| Multi-domain mechanism changes, temporal/spatial ICA and auxiliary recovery | Exact known-DAG/intervention, linked-series, rank/span and invariant-mechanism premises recorded for relevant positive results. | Unavailable observations remain unavailable. Empirical batch labels or depth proxies are not substitutes for causal interventions, temporal linkage or verified auxiliary modulation. |
| Statistical matching, unlinked regression, OT and manifold coupling | Preserved prior theorem/partial-identification results plus expanded citation review; new transport E/E2 is distinguished from optimizer-defined couplings. | Conditional independence, monotonicity, anchors or correct geometry can identify restricted targets. A preferred coupling without those assumptions is not the biological joint. No duplicate benchmark of an equivalent objective. |
| Practical single-cell count/mosaic integration | pi-VAE, inVAE, scShift, GLUE, MIDAS and related sources distinguish estimator architecture from population theorem; count-kernel proof gaps and required guidance/correspondences recorded. | A useful empirical integration method need not identify shared biology. No paired labels, cell types or additional modalities introduced to make its supervision available. Count-factor software without a shared-recovery theorem is adjacent, not a refuted method. |
| Observed-count DAG methods | PT-SEM/RCINB and related primary routes inspected, with exact restrictions and available algorithm scope. | These target observed-variable DAGs under conditional-model restrictions, not automatically cross-assay latent recovery. Missing patient/intervention variables and exponential exact-search costs are method-specific; greedy alternatives are not falsely declared impossible. |
| Nonidentification/partial identification | Frozen augmented-observation Lean countermodels and P01 bounds retained; new unknown-NB and nonlinear-premise obstructions added. | These refute specified model-class/target claims only. Negative experimental fits never replace equal-observation/different-target countermodels. |

No new method has been selected using heldout pairing, state labels, graph truth or test scores. The three experiments alter the underlying count estimation or mixture structure and test distinct mechanisms; they are not arbitrary combinations chosen until an edge appears. Future work should resolve the named model/estimator dependencies before any new fitting plan. No additional data acquisition or benchmark generation is authorized by this review.
