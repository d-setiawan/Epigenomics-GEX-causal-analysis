# Historical C03 proposal: tensor-free finite-mixture moments

This preliminary proposal is superseded by the [approved C03 validation plan](C03_MEITD_VALIDATION.md). The [C03 implementation and protocol](../experiments/C03/) exist; independent pre-fit review has passed. This document is retained as proposal history, not the executable protocol.

The purpose would be to test whether a second, theorem-supported estimator improves **within-assay** count prediction under the same finite-class conditional-independence model studied by C01. This is not a cross-assay graph or a recovery claim. The estimator is Zhang–Kileel's conditional-mixture moment ALS, distinct from C01's likelihood EM and previous count ICA moment runs. See [primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/2210.14386) Theorems 2.8 and 3.9 and the consolidated disposition.

A potential frozen design uses the existing permitted training matrices, same 30/300 feature panels, r=4 classes, original and independently column-permuted nulls, and prespecified independent starts. Fit only training samples; use fixed training-only normalization per feature, carry means/scales for inverse transformation, and retain cell-level likelihood of a **new explicitly specified product-Poisson prediction model** p(x)=Σ_k w_k ∏_j Pois(x_j; μ_jk). The moments theorem identifies weights and conditional means under conditional independence, not a Poisson observation family. This likelihood is an additional predictive assumption, not an implication of the theorem; use the same pre-existing calibration/evaluation separation and C01 comparator. Do not score unobserved simulation state as truth. Separate the finite-class target from the hypothesis that both assays share proportions and latent classes; no cross-assay coupling is established by separate fits.

The [pinned source audit](../reviews/MEITD_SOURCE_AUDIT.md) finds that repository defaults do not activate all paper ALS++ heuristics; a faithful experiment must specify each such choice. Paper experiments use moment orders 1–4, τᵢ=(J−i)!/J!, standardized input and stopping threshold 10⁻⁴ for changes to weights and means. Orders 2 and 3 suffice for the generic population dimension inequality for r≤4,J=30/300, but comparison must preselect which orders and why, before inspecting results. Prespecify starts, maximum iterations, stopping, regularization, handling of zero-variance features and bounded runtime after inspecting implementation. Require finite normalized nonnegative weights, strictly positive retained components where division needs it, and finite nonnegative inverse-transformed Poisson rates. Record invalidity and failed/singular starts; do not silently floor or reinterpret. If source modification or fallback is required, document and review it before use. The source API, constraints and dependencies are in the audit; caller-supplied simplex QP, compatibility, initialization, exact zero-variance handling, treatment of source fallback and actual hardware/time estimate remain unresolved.

Performance would report held-out log score against C01 r=1/r=4 and the column null, with fit diagnostics, class-weight instability and optimization failures. A good score does not prove conditional independence, latent class biology or equal proportions across selected assays; feature selection and local QC can change the selected population. The theorem is population-level and local convergence does not guarantee a good finite-sample optimum. A runnable fit requires coordinator approval of a complete executable and sealed plan; none is approved by this note.
