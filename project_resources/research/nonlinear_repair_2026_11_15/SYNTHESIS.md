# Scientific synthesis: repaired identification classes and existing-count validation

2026-11-15. Scientific proofs, all three experimental reviews, navigation and integrity checks are complete; the mechanical record is `reviews/final_validation.json`. This document separates population identification, estimator performance and biological interpretation.

## What was repaired

The original unrestricted all-measurable domain-variability premise has a literal common-null-point obstruction. Merely changing its quantifiers to almost-everywhere statements does not resolve simultaneous bisection for finitely many atomless style laws. These are scoped objections to that premise, not a proof that nonlinear recovery is universally impossible. The [prior recheck](../nonlinear_recheck_2026_11_14/) preserves the exact source comparisons and independent objections.

The [replacement results A–D](theory/REPAIR_PROPOSALS.md) impose substantive restrictions and give admissible examples. A restricts a scalar content/style encoder; differing style means then remove its style coefficient. C assumes a common known nonlinear inverse link and a sufficiently rich style-mean contrast space; an annihilator recovers a content quotient up to an invertible linear map. Neither follows from merely observing assay IDs. D gives nonlinear auxiliary-variable identification of sufficient statistics up to an invertible affine transformation from full count laws: a common injective decoder, positive-support conditional exponential family and full-rank auxiliary contrasts in both competing models are required. Its full-count Poisson argument avoids substituting binary detection probabilities for an identified intensity law. It does not establish componentwise recovery or a genomic causal graph.

B provides a distinct, executable finite shared-state alternative. Product-Poisson mixtures identify distinct rate atoms and their positive masses within an assay. Exactly common, distinct class masses across the selected assay populations align these anonymous states. Identified posterior probabilities do not reveal each noisy cell's realized state. A cross-assay joint count law additionally requires a shared-state structural coupling and conditional independence of assay noise; marginal equality alone does not supply either.

The [fixed-known-shape negative-binomial bridge](theory/FIXED_SHAPE_NB_BRIDGE.md) extends arbitrary nonnegative intensity-law identification, including zero-coordinate patterns, through Poisson-gamma mixing and nonvanishing log-gamma characteristic functions. No moment assumption is needed. The [unknown-shape countermodel](theory/UNKNOWN_SHAPE_NB_COUNTERMODEL.md) shows why learning dispersion cannot inherit that claim without new restrictions: shape-one constant mean one and shape-two uniformly mixed mean on (0,2) produce the same count law. This does not refute every finite-atomic or restricted unknown-dispersion model.

[E](theory/COMP_COUNT_SHARED_TRANSPORT.md) gives a second substantive nonlinear count-compatible shared-state repair: independent non-Gaussian sources with unit variances and no signed-permutation symmetry, invertible linear mixing of log rates, and a known Poisson observation channel identify the unique linear log-rate transport and corresponding nonlinear rate map. [E2](theory/COMP_TALL_MATRIX_COROLLARY.md) allows unequal feature counts and full-column-rank assay matrices: covariance identifies each log-rate support subspace, and transport is unique on those subspaces. An arbitrary ambient extension is not identified. Assay features need not have matching biological meanings. Common source distribution, independent components and absent source symmetries remain strong unverified biological assumptions. These are population theorems; no finite-count estimator consistency or empirical E/E2 validation is claimed.

A late direct sparse-ICA citation search found a further distinct analytic-source theorem. The [exact countermodel](reviews/KINKS_SMOOTHNESS_COUNTEREXAMPLE.md), independently [reviewed](reviews/KINKS_INDEPENDENT_CORRESPONDENCE_REVIEW.md), disproves its general signed-affine conclusion: an analytic conjugate of Laplace reflection preserves a fixed admissible kinked prior but is nonlinear. The countermodel is componentwise and therefore does not refute the weaker target up to arbitrary componentwise transformations. We also proved and independently checked a substantive restricted alternative: any global analytic diffeomorphism preserving exact iid standard Laplace coordinates is a signed permutation. Combined with full product-Poisson intensity-law identification and a known log link, this identifies a global square analytic source representation within an assay up to signed permutation. Its exact prior is nonvacuous but unverified here; separate assays retain separate sign/permutation ambiguities. No count-native estimator or cross-assay alignment is validated by this result. The original proof note's pending-review sentence is superseded by its independent review; reviewed source bytes are preserved.

## What the count experiments establish

The three experiments use the same permitted cached biological and simulation inputs, with frozen training/calibration splits, controls and scoring. These are reused-world model applications, not newly generated theorem-satisfying benchmarks. Original feature selection used the full training pool; the later fit/calibration split does not undo that dependency. Assay-local filtering also differs from the paired all-assay evaluator population. Unit-exposure models identify effective intensity and may track depth rather than biological state.

[C01](experiments/C01/RESULTS.md) executed 24 model/control arms with 864 primary restarts, product-Poisson and fixed-shape-two NB mixtures, and training-only bootstrap/matching gates. Twenty-one multi-state arms abstained; three real-data null arms selected a single component. No cross-assay shared-state candidate survived. Even the simulated chain 300-feature Poisson arm with stable individual assays had incompatible weight intervals. This is evidence against successful recovery by this fitted finite-mixture procedure under these settings, not proof that the observation design is universally nonidentifying. Real histone assays independently preferred one component, simulated models hit the K=4 grid boundary, and depth associations, unstable bootstrap recovery and local optima preserve several competing explanations. Within-assay predictive improvements do not establish shared-state alignment.

[C02](experiments/C02/RESULTS.md) applied the official restricted latent-class DCRL estimator in 36 applications and 108 starts. Six applications failed because fitted zero rates assigned zero probability to observed training rows; 16 selected fits were finite-converged and 14 finite-nonconverged. Fifteen of 30 finite fitted loading patterns passed the implemented sufficient-pattern certificate, but a certificate on an estimated pattern does not verify the true data-generating assumptions. On comparable full-panel heldout views, 29 of 30 finite applications scored worse than the C01 four-component comparator. Different complexity and optimization prevent interpreting that comparison as a theorem ranking. No shared latent causal graph was recovered or scored against unrelated feature truth.

C03 applies the distinct Zhang–Kileel off-diagonal moment estimator with three starts to the same 36 assay/panel/control applications. It uses the author's baseline solver with an independently checked simplex-QP adapter, not the complete ALS++ performance procedure. All 108 starts completed; 34 met convergence criteria and 74 did not. The final 36 selected fits comprise 13 converged and 23 nonconverged. Independent fit replay checked all final objectives and selections. [Evaluation and full independent replay](experiments/C03/RESULTS.md) are complete: 42 of 54 metric views have finite predictive scores and 12 are negative-infinite. Against the C01 four-component comparator, two full-panel views improve, 23 have worse finite scores and 11 are negative-infinite. All outcomes remain in the record; the moment objective is not a predictive-likelihood objective. An evaluator import-order runtime guard failure occurred before test access; its narrowly reviewed admission preserves all frozen numerical sources, fits and scoring, while recording the additional one-thread MKL/OpenMP libraries. A provenance repair is not a model improvement.

No threshold was widened to create an edge. Paired/state truth was used only by the sealed C01 evaluator; C02/C03 do not access it. All failures and negative controls remain recorded. The independent reviews distinguish numerical replay from rerunning every stochastic optimizer or proving global optimality.

## Coverage and remaining scientific boundaries

The [theorem/algorithm matrix](THEOREM_ALGORITHM_DISPOSITION.md), [coverage status](LITERATURE_COVERAGE_STATUS.md), [search protocol](COVERAGE_PROTOCOL.md), [chronological log](SEARCH_LOG.md) and [branch citation crosswalk](BRANCH_CITATION_CROSSWALK.md) give the reproducible evidence. The original eight families are mapped explicitly to current work and preserved prior results. The expanded query corpus contains 2,253 appearances and 2,065 OpenAlex work IDs, not necessarily distinct publications. Focused citation passes are additional and bounded; caps, missing abstracts, version duplication and retrieval limitations are recorded. Metadata accounting is not theorem review, and neither proves exhaustive literature coverage.

Missing observations, unverified model assumptions and unresolved estimator adaptations are different outcomes. Paired-view, intervention, temporal, known-anchor and common-coordinate methods cannot silently acquire those observations. By contrast, count-compatible population compositions such as E/E2 are scientifically viable conditional routes whose finite-sample estimation remains unresolved. Existing Gaussian-decoder software does not automatically implement those theorems. Empirical unpaired integrators can be useful without identifying the biological coupling; their learned coupling must not be relabeled as an established causal target.

The current evidence supports several nonvacuous conditional identification results, explicit obstructions to overly broad premises and count-channel claims, and executed negative or limited finite-sample results. It does not identify the real cross-assay biological joint, the realized common state of each cell, a latent biological DAG or a genomic regulatory graph. Further work would need an estimator justified for an accepted model class and evidence for its assumptions within the existing information contract, rather than extra hidden labels or an optimizer-selected coupling presented as truth.
