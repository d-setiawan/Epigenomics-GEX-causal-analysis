# C02: author-source discrete latent count model

Status: all 36 applications and 108 starts executed; evaluation and independent numerical review complete. This is a faithful application of the author observed-data API with frozen defaults and fixed two binary latent dimensions, not confirmation of the population theorem or a reproduction of the paper's benchmark results.

Sixteen applications met the recorded source convergence/optimizer criteria, 14 retained finite models with nonconvergence diagnostics, and six failed. All six failures were S1 histone applications: all 18 associated starts produced finite nonnegative parameters but assigned zero probability to some source-fit observations, hence had negative-infinite attained training likelihood. Across those applications the unsupported training-row counts were 7,16,12,21,37 and 105. These are estimator support failures under the frozen penalized source algorithm, not a proof that no suitable latent model exists. No rate floors or row deletion repaired them.

Among the 30 finite selected fits, 15 estimated Q matrices passed the prescribed combinatorial design check and 15 failed; 29 had all four latent configurations assigned positive fitted mass. A fitted-Q pass is not evidence that the true measurement graph, faithfulness, positivity, genericity or conditional independence assumptions hold. The study did not infer or validate a latent DAG or cross-assay alignment. S1 was especially weak: six of 12 applications failed and five of its six finite fits retained nonconvergence diagnostics.

The independent reviewer recomputed all 90 successful-start training likelihoods and penalized objectives, all 30 selected training/calibration scores and Q/default/bound/status checks, and all 46 C02 plus 108 C01 comparator metric views. All 36 evaluation outputs and nine test-input bindings matched. Every evaluated C02 score view was finite; the prefit negative-infinity edge-case correction was nevertheless necessary and preserved in the v1/v2 history. The review did not rerun every stochastic optimization or certify global optima. See [independent review](../../reviews/c02_independent_postfit/REVIEW.md).

Among the 30 finite applications on their full fitted-panel views, C02 improved test log score over C01's Poisson K=1 baseline in 22 and worsened it in 8. Against C01's unrestricted Poisson K=4 fit, it improved in 1 and worsened in 29. These are correlated descriptive comparisons between different model constraints, penalties and optimizers, not significance tests or a universal method ranking. Failed applications remain in the 36-application denominator and have no successful C02 prediction to compare.

No paired biological reference, hidden simulator state, causal truth or additional data was used. Current assay-local selection, count depth, parameter bounds and source optimization limit interpretation. The broader program remains active for the distinct moment-estimator application and final literature/synthesis audit.
